## Финальный промпт

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: original main hero creative for the Russian taxi brand Астро-Волга Такси landing page. Create a premium refined editorial 3D illustration with an airy pale icy blue and white palette, brand accents rich medium blue #0067b1 and crimson #c61730. Main subject: a beautiful modern white taxi sedan with subtle deep blue lower body accents, a small red taxi roof sign without letters, shown front-left three-quarter view, facing toward left, elegant realistic proportions and glossy material, occupying the lower center and right of the composition. Behind it, a tasteful compact miniature city scene with classical pale neoclassical architecture, simple houses, trees and a few soft blue clouds. This is a conceptual city, no recognizable landmark or geographic labels. Integrate a flowing blue route-like ribbon visually around the scene. Art direction: sophisticated semi-realistic illustration combining detailed automobile rendering with a clean miniature architectural diorama; premium commercial quality, restrained color, soft daylight, elegant shadows, no childish cartoon look. Square composition with car entirely visible, ample breathing room around all silhouettes, no cropped wheels or buildings. Background must be truly transparent, with only soft contact shadows beneath the car and miniature city. No text, no lettering, no brand logos, no watermarks, no UI, no people.
